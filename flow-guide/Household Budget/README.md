# Life admin, handled

**For everyone.** “Close the month without a spreadsheet marathon, and keep my bank files off someone else’s server.”

| For | What lasts | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax · budget · insurance · travel | Saves time, Private | Free to start |

1. **Make a copy of the Household Budget.** Choosing this path on the **Paths** screen already made it: this folder is your copy. See [Using Flow ▸ Keep the folder portable](x-flow://handbook/Using%20Flow#Keep%20the%20folder%20portable).
2. **Replace the sample statements with your own.** Put your bank’s CSV exports in `statements/` and set your plan in [Budget Profile](Budget%20Profile.md). See [Using Flow ▸ Keep the folder portable](x-flow://handbook/Using%20Flow#Keep%20the%20folder%20portable).
3. **Run Jobs to redraw this month’s totals.** **Run Jobs** recomputes the totals from your files, and the charts redraw. See [Night Shift Handbook ▸ Tables are inputs; Jobs repeat the work](x-flow://handbook/Night%20Shift%20Handbook#Tables%20are%20inputs%3B%20Jobs%20repeat%20the%20work).

**What lasts:** the month closes in minutes, and nothing leaves this Mac.

The same three steps work for the **Tax Advisor**, **Insurance Renewal** and **Travel Planner** workspaces. See the finished page first: [Household Budget](Household%20Budget.md).

## This folder is yours

Flow made this copy for you in a folder of its own. Replace the sample inputs with yours and change anything; the Jobs run only when you ask. The Flow Guide keeps the original Household Budget.
