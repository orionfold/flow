# Quarterly Close

**Fictional Meridian Software, FY2026.** The full-year sample is an invented planning scenario, not Orionfold performance or an audited filing. Revenue is recognised revenue in USD millions; operating income excludes financing and tax.

## Quarters

Use the table’s **Open in the table editor** control, or **View ▸ Edit Table**. Try changing Q4’s revenue from **5.7** to **6.0**. Then open [[Business Review]] and choose **Settings ▸ Night Shift ▸ Run now**. Annual revenue becomes **$19.5M**. The input table stays yours to edit; the report is regenerated.

## Field guide

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| revenue_m | Recognised revenue for the quarter, USD millions |
| gross_margin_pct | Quarterly gross profit divided by revenue, percent |
| customers | Paying customers at quarter end |
| operating_income_m | Quarterly operating profit or loss, USD millions |

Enter plain numbers in the numeric columns; amounts are USD millions. The living review recomputes the totals. Review the authored letter and annual narrative after the data changes.

To change how the numbers are calculated, open [[Business Review Refresh]] and choose **File ▸ Edit Definition…**. Document title and tags stay in document properties; no YAML editing is needed for this exercise.
