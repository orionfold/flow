# Investor Update

**A founder's monthly update, drafted from the watch.** The Night Shift keeps the market sources fresh; **Expand with Sources** drafts the market section with a citation on every claim. When it reads right, choose **File ▸ Publish…** and **PowerPoint** for the deck investors expect, or **PDF**.

| Section | Draws on | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The month in numbers | Your metrics workbook, imported with File ▸ Import… | Your figures |
| What moved in the market | The watch's sources in sources/current | Drafted with sources |
| What we decided | Research Decisions | Your words |
