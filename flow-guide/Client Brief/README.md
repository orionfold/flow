# The recurring client brief

**For consultants.** “Turn a pile of client material into a brief I’d sign, and do it again next month without starting over.”

| For | What lasts | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consultants · analysts · fractional operators | Saves time, Quality | Flow Pro, Flow Import, Flow Publish |

1. **Import last month’s PDFs, decks and sheets.** **File ▸ Import…** reads PDF, PowerPoint, Excel and Word into `client-files/` as Markdown you can search and cite. See [Using Flow ▸ Read, work and keep the result](x-flow://handbook/Using%20Flow#Read%2C%20work%20and%20keep%20the%20result).
2. **Draft the brief with sources, and approve it.** **Expand with Sources** drafts *This month* with a citation on every claim; **Review Changes** lets you keep what is right. Choose a model on this Mac for client material.
3. **Publish to PDF or Word; refresh next month.** **Publish** writes the file the client receives. Next month, import the new files and **Run Jobs**: the changed figures redraw.

**What lasts:** every figure in the brief points at the client’s own file, and next month starts from this one.

See the finished page first: [Client Brief](Client%20Brief.md).
