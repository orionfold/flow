## About these inputs

**Fictional ten-night trip for two travellers.** Every stay and price is an illustrative plan; no reservation, availability or current fare is represented.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Place | Your stop |
| Arrival and departure | Dates in YYYY-MM-DD |
| Nights | Count that reconciles to the dates |
| Budget eur | Total for the stay, not nightly price |
| Status | Planned, Shortlist or Confirmed after actual booking |

Keep real confirmation references in your private copy. Do not ship or share passport details or payment credentials in a template.
