# Worked example — Decision Brief

## Input

> Our support desk contract renews on 1 December. Deskly has been fine,
> but the price goes up 20%. Helpline would connect to billing as well as
> the CRM, which the support lead says would save an hour a day, but
> moving takes a month and the team is short-handed until January.
> Finance's quote puts Deskly at $30 a seat after the rise and Helpline at
> $40.

## Output

### Decision

Do we renew Deskly before 1 December, or move to Helpline?

### Context

Deskly's price rises 20% at the 1 December renewal. The team is
short-handed until January, which matters for any move.

### Options

| Option | What it means | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renew Deskly | Stay at $30 a seat | No move while short-handed | Price up 20% |
| Move to Helpline | $40 a seat, a month to move | Connects to billing too; an hour a day saved | A month's move while short-handed |

### Recommendation

No recommendation yet: the text weighs Helpline's hour a day against a
month's move during a short-handed period without choosing. It turns on
whether that hour a day is worth a month's move before January.

### Risks

- Renewing for a long term locks in the 20% rise.
- Moving now lands the month's work on a short-handed team.

### Sources

- Finance's quote for both desks.
- The support lead's estimate of an hour a day.
