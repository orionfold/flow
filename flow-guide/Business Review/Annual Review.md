# Annual Review

**Meridian Software · fictional FY2026 operating review · draft for the annual meeting**

## Expansion funded the year; new-customer growth needs attention

We finished the sample year at $19.2M revenue and $0.4M operating income. Growth came with a tradeoff: the company served larger accounts while the self-serve business received less attention. The FY2027 plan should preserve the improvement in margin and put an owner against customer acquisition.

## The operating choices

| Choice | Evidence | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prioritise expansion | Revenue up 34.3%; sample NRR 118% | Acquisition may be masked by larger renewals |
| Keep hiring measured | 19 people at year end, versus 15 prior year | Two enablement projects slipped |
| Improve service reliability | Two incidents totalling 4.1 hours | Staged releases take more preparation |

These are fictional management observations, not independently audited results. Quarterly gross margin starts at 79% and reaches 83%; the revenue-weighted full-year margin is 81.2%. The second half contributes 56.8% of annual revenue, not two-thirds.

## What missed

Partner enablement started a quarter late because no owner was assigned. Two customer cohorts lacked retrospectives, leaving their outcomes unmeasured. Self-serve revenue fell from $2.3M to $1.5M in this scenario; the team’s explanation is a hypothesis until acquisition and retention are separated.
