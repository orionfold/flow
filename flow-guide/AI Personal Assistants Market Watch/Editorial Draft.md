# Editorial Draft

Use this short explanatory draft to inspect proofreading in a content-focused surface. Prepare an actual editorial draft here when useful; the action preserves Markdown and proposes text for review.

## Draft for editing

A useful market brief separates the claim from the check. It names the task a person wants to complete, the evidence available and what remains unknown. A product announcement can change the questions worth asking without settling the answer. Clear dates, comparable units and an explicit return to the source make a short report easier to trust.
