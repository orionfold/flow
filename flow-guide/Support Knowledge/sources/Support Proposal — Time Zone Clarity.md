# Explain display time before users change a booking

**Authored sample proposal for the fictional Beacon Scheduling product. Not published.**

## What prompted it

SK-05 and SK-12 describe two versions of the same confusion: whether a time-zone choice changes only one person's display or reschedules a booking. Read [SK-05](Support%20Cases%20%E2%80%94%202026-09-05.md) and [SK-12](Support%20Cases%20%E2%80%94%202026-09-12.md) before reviewing the wording.

## Replace the ambiguous sentence

**Existing draft:** “Change the time zone to see the right meeting time.”

**Proposed wording:** “Your display time zone changes how booking times appear to you. It does not, by itself, move the booking for other people. To reschedule a booking, use the booking's reschedule action and confirm the new time with the participants.”

## Add one example

An organizer books a meeting for 09:00 in their local zone. A colleague travelling in another zone should confirm the equivalent local display time before attending. Changing that colleague's display preference is different from moving the scheduled meeting.

The final help article should use actual zone names and a verified date-specific conversion. This proposal deliberately supplies no conversion until someone checks it.
