# Quarterly Review

**Fictional Meridian Software · Q3 FY2026 · management review**

## Revenue grew 18.2%; margin improved two points

Q3 revenue of $5.2M follows $4.4M in Q2. Gross margin rises from 80% to 82%, and operating income moves from a $0.2M loss to a $0.3M profit. This is a useful change in the business, but one quarter does not establish repeatability.

| Metric | Q2 | Q3 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognised revenue, $M | 4.4 | 5.2 | +18.2% |
| Gross margin | 80% | 82% | +2pp |
| Operating income, $M | -0.2 | 0.3 | +$0.5M |
| Customers at quarter end | 419 | 433 | +14 net |

## Decision for the next meeting

Request acquisition and expansion cohorts before moving budget. The current close has financial results but no reliable attribution. Assign Growth to provide a channel view and Finance to reconcile it to recognised revenue.
