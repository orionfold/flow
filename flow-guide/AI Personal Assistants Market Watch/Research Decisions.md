# Research Decisions

Decisions are deliberately undecided. Jobs can help expand analysis; it does not choose a disposition, mark a human check, contact anyone or make an investment decision.

## Hypotheses worth testing

| ID | Hypothesis | Alternative explanation | Evidence to seek | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Clear correction and return paths matter more than an extra action capability for a recurring briefing. | Better initial answer quality could remove much of the checking burden. | Same task, independently checked output, correction minutes and second-use result. | undecided |
| H02 | Distribution through an existing work or device surface lowers adoption effort. | Switching and permission setup may outweigh convenient entry. | A first-use task, setup steps and where useful output goes afterward. | undecided |
| H03 | Explicit execution locality and permissions improve trust. | Users may prioritize convenience and only inspect controls after a failure. | A permission/recovery probe and interviews about actual choices. | undecided |

These are analyst hypotheses, not observations about the eight products.

## Experiment protocol

Prepare a small synthetic inbox/calendar containing one ambiguous appointment, one superseded plan and one irrelevant message. Write the correct briefing before testing. Give each product the same task and only the intended access. Observe whether it asks about the ambiguity, cites the right evidence and preserves a usable result. Return later with one changed input. Record setup and correction time, unsupported claims, approval interruptions, completed output and useful second-cycle changes. All measurements begin blank until observed.
