# Client Proposal — Meridian Retail

**Fictional proposal for adaptation · no offer has been sent or accepted**

**Engagement:** AI operations readiness assessment **Prepared by:** Northlight Advisory · **Valid through:** October 15, 2026

Meridian's merchandising team is drowning in narrative work: weekly trade summaries, vendor briefs, and seasonal strategy documents, each assembled by hand from the same underlying data. This proposal scopes a six-week engagement to redesign that document pipeline around reviewed automation — every generated draft passes through a human approval gate and leaves an auditable record.

![Meridian's flagship-format produce floor, the pilot environment](assets/retail-interior.jpeg)

## What Meridian gets

- A mapped inventory of the fourteen recurring documents the merchandising team produces, with effort and error baselines
- A redesigned workflow for the top five documents, with automation proposals reviewed and approved by the document owners themselves
- A working measurement of time saved, verification time spent, and error rates before and after
- A written playbook the team owns after we leave

## How we work

Northlight does not install a black box and leave. The pilot will define which proposed changes need owner review and how the team records its decisions. The merchandising team keeps judgment; the pipeline keeps the receipts.

## Assumptions and exclusions

- Meridian provides read access to the current document repository and two hours weekly from each document owner
- Vendor data integrations beyond the existing exports are out of scope
- Legal review of AI-generated customer-facing copy remains with Meridian's counsel
