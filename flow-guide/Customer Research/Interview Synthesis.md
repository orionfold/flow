# Interview Synthesis

**Fictional research synthesis · sample designed to demonstrate coding**

## One coded need is a prompt for research, not proof of demand

The register contains six enterprise and five self-serve interviews. Five enterprise entries prioritise an approval record; one prioritises local access. The self-serve entries split between portable files and faster review.

## How the next study should improve

Recruit people who abandoned a trial and people whose work happens outside document software. Ask them to walk through a recent task before naming features. Preserve the original phrasing, record consent and dates, and code the same need consistently.

This sample has no original transcripts and cannot support verbatim quotes. [[Research Brief]] shows its counted findings, while [[Market Hypotheses]] keeps the untested interpretations visible.
