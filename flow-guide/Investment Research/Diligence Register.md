## About these inputs

**Fictional private-company diligence exercise.** No securities transaction or investment recommendation is made. The observations below are invented management representations, not verified facts.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Question | A decision-relevant uncertainty |
| Status | Open or Checked |
| Owner | Person who will obtain evidence |
| Evidence | What is supplied or missing |

Keep valuations, customer claims and verification status separate. A completed checklist does not make an investment suitable.
