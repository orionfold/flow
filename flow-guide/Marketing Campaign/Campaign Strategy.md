# Campaign Strategy

**Fictional October editorial plan · owner: Mei**

## Help an operator finish a review with confidence

The audience is a working professional with a recurring document to maintain. Each piece should give that reader something they can use: a question, an example, a checklist or a method.

| Piece | Reader’s job | Evidence before publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verification study | Decide what needs checking | Method, sample and denominators |
| Review checklist | Prepare a repeatable review | A worked example |
| Customer workflow | Learn a credible practice | Permission and a source transcript |
| Approval questions | Surface unresolved assumptions | Concrete questions, no invented quote |

## Distribution is a separate decision

The calendar holds drafts and review dates. Owners choose the site, newsletter or social channel after approval. This folder has no publishing integration.

## A useful learning loop

Record replies that describe actual use, requests for examples and returning readers. Separate them from impressions. Before the next month, compare the response with the purpose assigned to each piece. The operational calendar is in [[Campaign Desk]].
