## Retained finding

Custom Agents use explicit resource grants. Shared-agent answers can expose information beyond a caller's direct access. Duplicates drop inaccessible resources and require connection reauthentication.

## Limits

Stated permission design, not a security audit or vulnerability finding.

## Decision relevance

Make adopted methods separate from adopted credentials and access grants.
