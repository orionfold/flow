# Investment Memo — Larkspur Robotics

**Round:** Series A · **Check under consideration:** $1.5M of $12M
**Status:** partner review · **Recommendation:** proceed to diligence

Larkspur builds inspection robots for mid-size food processing plants —
a segment the large integrators ignore and the point-solution startups
cannot reach because the sales motion runs through plant managers, not
CTOs. The company is default-alive, growing on operator-led sales, and
asking for capital to industrialize manufacturing, not to find
product-market fit.

![Larkspur's rail-mounted inspection unit on a customer's processing line](assets/inspection-robot.jpeg)

## Thesis

1. **The buyer is underserved on purpose.** Integrators chase
   $10M+ automotive contracts; Larkspur's average contract is $180K
   with a nine-week sales cycle. The competition is not other robots —
   it is a clipboard.
2. **The product compounds.** Every inspection run enriches the defect
   library; the defect library improves detection; detection wins the
   next plant in the same network. Three of the last five customers
   arrived by referral from an existing plant's quality director.
3. **The team has lived the problem.** Both founders ran plant
   operations; the CTO shipped vision systems at an integrator for
   nine years.

## The numbers

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q1 2026 | Q3 2026 |
|:-------|--------:|--------:|--------:|
| ARR | $410K | $890K | $1.6M |
| Plants live | 6 | 11 | 19 |
| Gross margin | 41% | 48% | 54% |
| Net revenue retention | — | 118% | 127% |
| Months of runway | 14 | 11 | 16 |

Margin expansion tracks the shift from bespoke installs to the
standardized rail-mounted unit introduced in January.

## Risks, stated plainly

- **Hardware margin ceiling.** Even standardized, hardware caps gross
  margin in the 50s; the software attach (defect analytics, $2K/month)
  must carry long-term margin, and attach is 63% today, unproven at
  scale.
- **Key-person concentration in sales.** The CEO has personally closed
  16 of 19 plants. The two hires made to replicate the motion have one
  closed deal between them. This is the diligence question.
- **Regulatory tailwind could stall.** The FSMA enforcement wave
  driving urgency is an assumption about a government schedule.

## Recommendation

Proceed to diligence with focus on the sales-motion transfer. Price is
fair at $48M post; the compounding defect library is the asset the
market is not yet pricing.
