# Research Mandate

## The question

Which personal-assistant tasks are becoming practical to delegate, through which distribution surfaces, with what checking effort, cost exposure and control?

| Choice | Starting value |
| --- | --- |
| Operator lens | Industry analyst |
| Audience | A reader deciding what merits closer investigation |
| Market | AI Personal Assistants |
| Geography and language | US / English; qualify all exceptions |
| Cohort | Eight selected products, not the whole market |
| Baseline | Public sources retrieved September 15, 2026 |
| Current task to understand | Maintain a trustworthy daily briefing and return to its evidence |
| Cadence | Manual; consider a weekly cycle after proving useful repeat work |
| Materiality | A changed access boundary, durable output, recurring-task contract, availability condition or comparable offer |
| Tolerable uncertainty | Published claims may guide questions; consequential conclusions require additional evidence |
| Delivery | Private working document; no automatic communication, publishing or trading |

Change these choices to match your work. This file supplies operator context; it does not establish competitor facts.

## Choose a useful lens

| Lens | Decision | Desired output | Missing evidence to seek |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Industry analyst | Has the basis of competition changed? | Qualified market map and change brief | Repeat-task outcomes and correction effort |
| Market entrant | Which narrow task or trust boundary is worth testing? | Falsifiable pilot with substitutes and a stop condition | Customer demand and willingness to switch/pay |
| Investor | Which distribution and cost mechanisms merit diligence? | Business-model questions and evidence triggers | Adoption, retention, margins and compute economics |
| Journalist | What changed, for whom, and what can be demonstrated? | Claim ledger and source-linked explanatory draft | Independent demonstration and on-record responses |

The investor lens does not recommend a security or trade. The journalist lens does not contact anyone or publish. A feature gap alone does not establish demand for a new business. All four lenses use the same source ledger.

## Deliberate boundaries

Include knowledge-work, browser, mobile/OS and household assistants when they can plausibly help a person remember, plan, retrieve, decide or act. Keep general model benchmarks, coding-only agents and enterprise agent infrastructure outside this first cohort unless a specific personal task makes them relevant.

Consider search plus notes, calendar reminders, specialist apps, a manual process and human assistance as substitutes. The work matters more than the product category name.
