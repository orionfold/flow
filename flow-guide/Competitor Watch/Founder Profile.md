# Founder Profile

Describe your product before choosing competitors. These fields express your intent; they are not market facts.

| Lens | Your working answer |
| --- | --- |
| Product and customer | An AI product for solo founders; replace with your specific customer and task |
| Surface | Choose website, Mac app or web app |
| Decision this week | Which recurring customer task should our next useful release address? |
| Alternatives | An existing AI assistant, a folder of notes, a custom small app, or doing nothing |
| Evidence needed | Public product documentation, pricing units, release changes and one firsthand task comparison |
| Resource constraint | One small experiment before building a broad feature |
| Success measure | Time to a usable result, correction effort and willingness to return |

## Adapt the lens

A website founder can compare positioning, onboarding, useful content, conversion and pricing clarity. A Mac app founder can compare offline work, keyboard entry, document ownership, permissions and system integration. A web app founder can compare collaboration, shared data, deployment, reliability and usage costs.

Pick two or three dimensions that change this week's decision. Keep the rest as questions. Revisit this profile when your customer or product changes.
