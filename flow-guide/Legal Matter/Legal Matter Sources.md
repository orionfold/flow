## The two packet states

The **5 September** packet contains LM-01 through LM-05 and five events. The **12 September** packet adds LM-06 and one earlier event. The receipt's event date remains 28 August; the chronology therefore places it beside the receiving note, not after September correspondence.

The added record strengthens the need to reconcile the count. It does not automatically make either account true. Preserve the first packet so the reviewer can see what was available before the receipt arrived.
