# Easily co-author with any AI once, one-click publish as AI system everywhere

*This is a worked example of the method in [Press Release FAQ for AI project](Press%20Release%20FAQ%20for%20AI%20project.md). The press release announces launch day, and the FAQ answers the questions a careful customer would ask. It describes Flow 3.0, released on November 1, 2026, and AI Consulting Starter, which launches with it.*

## Press release

**Flow 3.0 helps subject matter experts turn industry experience into a reusable system for client work: define the method, apply it with AI, review the result and publish the deliverables from one maintained source.**

**November 1, 2026** — Orionfold today released Flow 3.0 for experts who want to put their knowledge to work in a service of their own, and launched AI Consulting Starter with it. A consultant may have a method for evaluating an AI investment. An architect may know how to compare deployment choices. Their customers need that expertise applied to the project in front of them, and applied again when the next project or a changed assumption arrives.

Flow brings the expert's methods, examples, sources and practical tools together in a Domain. The expert co-authors with AI, checks the assumptions and exceptions, and shapes an offering under their own brand. People can then use that knowledge directly in Flow.

Start with a result a customer needs and a clear standard for accepting it. The expert records the source requirements, assumptions, exceptions and examples that make the method useful. AI helps apply the method to current inputs. The expert checks the result before delivery and writes useful corrections back into the maintained source, so the next engagement starts from what was learned.

The same source can become a GitHub Pages website, a GitHub repository, a PDF, Office documents or an EPUB. An expert can share a practical guide, prepare client documents, publish a book and keep a versioned knowledge base, all from the same underlying work. Each format presents the material in the way it supports. Flow's native AI and interactive features stay in Flow; exported files are for reading and delivery.

Flow gives experts an opinionated starting point for choosing AI, and control when their work needs a different trade-off. You can keep the default route, choose a model yourself or adjust the routing rules for cost, quality, convenience and speed. Your source and review process stay the same when you change routes.

The document remains the center of the work. You choose which sources AI may use and where they are processed, inspect proposed edits and review the recorded checks before saving a result. Publishing follows your review of the destination and the material being shared.

AI Consulting Starter launches with Flow 3.0 as its first Domain. It distils nine years at Amazon leading product, solutions and business development across AWS, Alexa AI and Shopping. It introduces Amazon's mechanisms, including Working Backwards and the PR/FAQ, then applies them to help a business adopt AI. A consultant, or a leader adopting AI, can follow it from a first mechanism to a PR/FAQ for a real AI project and a plan to take a pilot to production.

**Founder quote:** “I want experts to bottle what they know in a system they can make their own. Co-authoring with AI is the beginning. The useful part is applying that expertise to a customer's work, improving it and being able to deliver it again.”

Experts subscribe to Flow to create, apply, maintain and publish their work. They provide services to their customers through their own commercial relationships. Domain publishing has no separate per-Domain fee beyond the applicable Flow subscription. Paid Domains and a marketplace come later.

**First step:** open AI Consulting Starter, choose one customer task, adapt the method to your expertise, review a useful result and publish it in the format your customer needs.

## Case studies

### A client: building on Relay

A client, the co-founder of a company building AI products for businesses and nonprofits, chose Relay for the expertise behind AI Native Business and for an open-source foundation they could adapt. Relay is the foundation of the client's platform, which learns a business from its own email, files and feedback, and works toward the owner’s goals. The client's product for small nonprofits is that platform focused on nonprofit operations, so it runs on Relay as well. The client's practical needs turned into product improvements. When they asked for private Git repository publishing, the feature was documented as shipped the following day. Flow is designed to make this path easier for subject matter experts. It pairs a familiar document interface with opinionated AI defaults: capture your methods, apply them to customer work, review the results, and publish useful deliverables under your own brand.

Relay is Orionfold's open-source project, and the client built on Relay rather than Flow. The case shows the path Flow is meant to make easier.

### Orionfold: building this Domain in Flow

AI Consulting Starter was written in Flow by its author, following the same loop it teaches, and launches with Flow 3.0.

- **A front page first.** The [README](README.md) was drafted with Chat in the author's voice. Every claim the author couldn't yet stand behind was marked `[check]` instead of being smoothed over.
- **An imported conversation became a reference.** An earlier AI chat about Amazon mechanisms was imported and restructured into [Amazon Mechanisms and Practices](Amazon%20Mechanisms%20and%20Practices.md). Each mechanism links to a public source, and the ones still without a source are marked.
- **Flow Elements make the method usable.** The [Mechanisms Primer](Mechanisms%20Primer.md) uses tables, a checklist of symptoms and a stepped figure, so readers can test their own business instead of only reading about it. The author reviewed it and accepted it as a living document.
- **The plan changed, and the source changed with it.** The path started with six documents. Review cut it to four, and every document that referred to the old path was updated to match.

The Domain launches as a living document with all four documents on its path, and no client result is claimed yet. The point of the case is the habit: draft with AI, review against your own standard, and write the correction back into the source.

## Customer FAQ

### Who is this for?

Two kinds of reader. A consultant who wants to help clients adopt AI, using a method they can apply again and again. And a leader adopting AI in their own business who wants a disciplined way to pick and test the right project. More broadly, Flow is for any subject matter expert who wants to put their experience into a system they can use to serve their own customers.

### What is a Flow Domain?

A Domain gathers your expertise into a collection of documents, examples, visual explanations and practical tools. Readers can explore it, ask questions and apply your method to their own work. Each Domain carries its author, version, sources and licence, and the work stays in readable Markdown files that you own.

### What is in AI Consulting Starter?

Four documents, in order:

1. [Mechanisms Primer](Mechanisms%20Primer.md): what a mechanism is, how to spot a missing one and how to build one.
2. [Amazon Mechanisms and Practices](Amazon%20Mechanisms%20and%20Practices.md): the publicly described mechanisms, and which ones this Domain uses.
3. [Press Release FAQ for AI project](Press%20Release%20FAQ%20for%20AI%20project.md): finding the right AI opportunity and describing it from the customer's side before you build.
4. [Pilot to Scale](Pilot%20to%20Scale.md): running a pilot that answers a real question and taking it into production.

### What is in it for me as an expert?

A reusable source of expertise for a service under your own brand. Your method, sources, examples, exceptions and acceptance standard stay together, so the next engagement builds on reviewed work instead of starting from another blank conversation. You own the service offer, the customer relationship and the final judgment.

### What is in it for my customer?

Your expertise, applied to their task. A client can receive a decision brief, a PR/FAQ or a workbook. A reader can use a guide or a book. Someone using Flow can work with the Domain's own tools. Your method and your judgment connect all of them.

### How is this different from publishing a PDF?

A PDF is one useful output. A Domain is the maintained source behind it: your methods, examples, sources and tools, with AI-assisted application and review in Flow. Published formats carry the elements they support. AI conversations and interactive figures don't become working AI inside a PDF or an ebook.

### How do I keep my own judgment in the result?

You remain the author. Before generating, write down the purpose, allowed sources, assumptions, examples, exceptions and the conditions that would make you reject the result. Flow proposes changes for you to review. Check the words, figures and references before you accept them. A polished answer or a passed check doesn't replace your judgment.

### Do I need to code?

No. You use Flow's import, Skills, review and publishing controls. You connect the model services you want to use and set up a publishing destination, and Flow shows the route before each run.

### How do I deliver work under my own brand?

Publish to a GitHub Pages site, a GitHub repository, PDF, Office documents or EPUB. Pick the format that suits the task: a website to explain your approach, a repository to share the source, or a document or book to deliver the work in a familiar form. Customers can use those files without Flow. Review the presentation, author information and sources before you share anything.

### What happens to confidential client material?

Your documents stay in your files. When you run AI, the route you choose decides where the selected content is processed. You can use local execution where it's supported, or a connected service, and Flow asks permission before a run leaves your Mac. Your provider's own terms decide how it handles what you send. Publishing is a separate step, so check files, destination and visibility before sharing.

### How do I choose which AI model to use?

Flow starts with sensible defaults and shows the route it would use. You can keep it, pick a provider and model yourself, or change the rules. No model is best at every task, and setup, usage charges and your review time all count toward the real cost of useful work.

### Is there a separate fee to publish a Domain?

No separate per-Domain publishing fee. Publishing is part of your Flow subscription. AI usage follows the terms of the model service you choose.

### Does publishing a Domain bring me customers?

No. Publishing gives people a way to find and use your work. You still need a clear offer, the right customers and a reason for them to choose your expertise. Flow doesn't guarantee leads, sales or recurring revenue.

### How does Flow fit with the tools I already use?

Often alongside them. Claude Code and Codex can run as routes inside Flow. General agents such as Hermes Agent and OpenClaw suit work across many tools and channels, while Flow organizes expert work around documents and their review. You can keep Obsidian or Notion for your wider knowledge base and try one project folder in Flow, or prepare reviewed content in Flow and finish the design in Canva. Check links, formatting and citations at every handoff.

### How do I tell whether it is helping?

Compare the whole job with how you work now: setup, source preparation, AI runs, review, corrections and publishing. Check the deliverable against your customer's acceptance standard, then repeat on the next task. A faster first draft only helps if the delivered work meets the standard at an effort and cost that suit your service.

### How do I get started?

Open the [Mechanisms Primer](Mechanisms%20Primer.md) and pick one real customer task. Write down the result you need, the inputs you're allowed to use, your acceptance standard and who makes the final decision. Work through the path to a [PR/FAQ](Press%20Release%20FAQ%20for%20AI%20project.md) for that task, review the result and deliver it in the right format. Record the corrections, and use the improved source next time.
