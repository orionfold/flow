## Retained finding

Browser history and other listed data stay local by default; required context can be sent for a request. Relevant temporary-thread context can be retained up to thirty days. First advanced-agent approval offers once/always/deny; site blocks and assistant-disable controls are documented.

## Limits

local defaults do not mean no network transmission. Removing temporary expiration changes retention. This is policy evidence, not an audit or observed network trace.

## Next useful check

Can the operator identify what was shared and revoke continuing access?
