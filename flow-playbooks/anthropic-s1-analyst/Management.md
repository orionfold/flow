## Director Terms

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering will provide that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, we will not have a classified board of directors, and all directors will be elected for annual terms. Each director’s term will continue until the next annual meeting and until the election and qualification of their successor, or their earlier death, resignation, or removal.

Following the Class F Sunset Time, our board of directors will shift to having a classified board of directors consisting of three classes of approximately equal size, each serving staggered three-year terms. At such time,

257

each of our directors would be assigned by the then-current board of directors to a class. If our Class T common stock remains outstanding, the Class T directors and the remaining directors shall be allocated among the classes so that each class includes a proportionate number of Class T directors and other directors. Upon expiration of the term of a class of directors, directors for that class would each be elected for a three-year term at the annual meeting of stockholders in the year in which that term expires. As a result, only one class of directors would be elected at each annual meeting of our stockholders, with the other classes continuing for the remainder of their respective three-year terms. Each director’s term would continue until the third annual meeting of stockholders following their election and until his or her successor has been elected and qualified, or until his or her earlier death, resignation, or removal. Any increase or decrease in the number of directors would be distributed among the three classes so that, as nearly as practicable, each class would consist of one- third of our directors.

Regardless of the class or classes of stock by which a director is elected, there is no limit to the number of terms a director may serve.

## Director Independence and Controlled Company Exemption

Our board of directors has undertaken a review of the independence of each director. Based on information provided by each director concerning their background, employment, and affiliations, our board of directors has determined that Mr. Hastings, Mr. Liddell, Dr. Narasimhan and Ms. Razavi do not have a relationship that would interfere with the exercise of independent judgment in carrying out the responsibilities of a director and that each of these directors is “independent” as that term is defined under the listing standards of the Nasdaq Global Select Market. In making these determinations, our board of directors considered the current and prior relationships that each non-employee director has with our company and all other facts and circumstances our board of directors deemed relevant in determining their independence, including the beneficial ownership of our capital stock by each non-employee director, and the transactions involving them described in the section titled “Certain Relationships and Related Person Transactions.”

We are considered a “controlled company” within the meaning of the Nasdaq Global Select Market corporate governance requirements because the LTBT, as the sole holder of our Class T common stock, generally has the

258

power to elect a majority of the authorized members of our board of directors. As a result, we may elect not to comply with certain corporate governance requirements, including:

•the requirement that a majority of our board of directors consist of independent directors;

•the requirement that our board of directors have a nominating and corporate governance committee

that is comprised entirely of independent directors with a written charter addressing the committee’s purpose and responsibilities; and

•the requirement that our board of directors have a compensation committee that is comprised entirely

of independent directors with a written charter addressing the committee’s purpose and responsibilities.

If we elect not to comply with certain requirements, you will not have the same protections afforded to stockholders of companies that are subject to all of the Nasdaq Global Select Market corporate governance requirements. Although we qualify as a “controlled company,” we do not expect to rely on this exemption except with respect to our NGC, which will not consist entirely of independent directors. Our board of directors currently consists of a majority of independent directors and our audit and compensation committees currently consist entirely of independent directors. In the event that we cease to be a “controlled company” and our Class A common stock continues to be listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, we will be required to comply with the provision that our NGC be comprised entirely of independent directors within the applicable transition period.
