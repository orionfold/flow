# Six roles, in the order they unblock work

This is not a headcount request. It is an argument about sequence: which role, hired when, removes the largest amount of work that currently cannot start.

![Planning, before the plan is pretty](assets/planning-desk.jpeg)

## The sequence, and why it is this one

| # | Role | Unblocks | Earliest useful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Senior backend | The ingest rewrite, currently one person deep | Immediately |
| 2 | Design engineer | Every surface waiting on a design pass | Immediately |
| 3 | Backend | Ingest, once the senior sets the shape | +6 weeks |
| 4 | QA lead | Release confidence, currently a manual pass | +8 weeks |
| 5 | Frontend | The editor backlog | +12 weeks |
| 6 | Data | Reporting, which is a spreadsheet today | +16 weeks |

The first two are not interchangeable with the rest. Both remove a **single-person dependency**, which is the only kind of hire that changes what the team can attempt rather than how fast it goes.

## What we are not hiring for

- **A manager.** Nine people is not the number that needs one.
- **A generalist.** Every role above names the work it unblocks; a role that cannot name one is a role we have not thought about hard enough.

## The cost, said plainly

Six roles at loaded cost is roughly $1.4M annualised, phased so that Q1 carries two and Q2 carries two more. Against the Q4 plan in [[Board Update — Q4 Outlook]] that is affordable; against a Q4 that misses, it is not, and roles 5 and 6 are the ones that would move.
