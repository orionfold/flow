# Shareholder Letter

**To the owners of Meridian Software · fictional FY2026 letter · draft**

We grew revenue 34.3% to $19.2M and reached $0.4M operating profit. The second half generated enough profit to offset the first-half losses. That is progress, but we will judge next year by whether it repeats.

## A small set of comparable numbers

| Metric | Fy2025 | Fy2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue, $M | 14.3 | 19.2 |
| Operating income, $M | -1.2 | 0.4 |
| Employees at year end | 15 | 19 |
| Revenue per year-end employee, $M | 0.953 | 1.011 |

Revenue per employee uses the year-end count; it is not a measure of individual performance. These sample management figures are not audited statements.

## What deserves attention

Larger renewals and expansions helped revenue while self-serve weakened. We should not let a healthy total conceal an acquisition problem. Our next commitment is a cohort review with a named owner, followed by a documented funding decision.

We also owe better execution on enablement. A project without an owner slipped, and two onboarding groups finished without a retrospective. Those misses belong in this letter because they affect our ability to repeat the year.
