## What to watch

**1. Does commercial momentum translate into durable demand?** Compare the next reported revenue and margin with this dated baseline, then investigate what changed. Record the reporting period, not just the headline date. Treat demand quality as an open question: this small source set does not establish customer returns, industry market share or a valuation.

**2. Does faster inference improve a real customer task?** Choose one measurable task before interpreting the product announcement. Compare useful completion, waiting time and total cost under the same model and input conditions. Keep benchmark claims separate from customer adoption and independently observed outcomes; do not infer universal superiority from a single configuration.

**3. Does the developer ecosystem stay open in practice?** Watch the agreement's status and the practical availability of model distribution and deployment choices. Record a dated example that supports or challenges the announced commitment. For a market entrant or journalist, the useful question is how choices change for builders, not whether an acquisition headline is inherently good or bad.
