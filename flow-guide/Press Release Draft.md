# Press Release Draft

**FOR REVIEW — embargoed until launch day**

*Draft copy, not yet proofread — the spelling errors below are deliberate,
so the proofread check has something to find.*

Flow 2.0 arives Septmber 30 with a simple promise: your documents
stay yours. Evrey file is plain text in folders you chose, every
automated change arives as a proposal you approve, and every approved
change leaves a reciept naming what ran, where it ran, and what it
cost.

![Field inspection hardware](assets/inspection-robot.jpeg)

*Field inspection work — the use case the release leads with.*

The team spent the sumer measuring rather than guessing. Search across
a ten-thousand-note libary answers in milliseconds and returns you to
the exact passage, verified against the file's actual bytes. Long
documents are improved part by part — never truncated, never silently
cut — with coverage reported in plain counts a reader can check. And
the AI runtime now ships inside the app itself: install nothing else,
pull a model, and your text never leaves the machine unless you say so.

"Professionals told us the same thing in every interview," said the
founder. "They do not distrust automation — they distrust silence. So
we built the oposite of silence: every change shows itself, asks
permision, and signs the visitor log on the way out."

Flow 2.0 is availble today for Mac. Pricing and the full feature
tour are at the product site.
