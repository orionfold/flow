# The investor update and the watch

**For founders.** “Write the investor update where every claim checks out, and stop reading every competitor’s blog.”

| For | What lasts | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founders | Saves time, Trust | Flow Pro, Flow Import, Flow Publish |

1. **Import the metrics workbook and last month’s deck.** **File ▸ Import…** brings Excel and PowerPoint in as Markdown you can read and edit. See [Using Flow ▸ Tables, charts and diagrams](x-flow://handbook/Using%20Flow#Tables%2C%20charts%20and%20diagrams).
2. **Watch competitors overnight; the Morning Briefing says what moved.** The **Keep sources fresh** Job watches the pages you name, and the Night Shift leaves a **Morning Briefing** of what changed. See [Night Shift Handbook ▸ Keep a useful document current](x-flow://handbook/Night%20Shift%20Handbook#Keep%20a%20useful%20document%20current).
3. **Publish PowerPoint or PDF, every claim sourced.** **Expand with Sources** drafts with citations you review, then **Publish** writes the deck or the PDF.

**What lasts:** the watching happens while you sleep, and every figure in the update points back to its source.

See the finished page first: [AI Personal Assistants Market Watch](AI%20Personal%20Assistants%20Market%20Watch.md).
