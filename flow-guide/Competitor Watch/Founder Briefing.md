# Founder Briefing

Prepare the material you want condensed here. The starting draft is a research briefing; replace it with selected accepted analysis when useful. This Summary Job reads its section and requests no source lookup.

## Briefing draft

A solo founder's competitor research should begin with a customer task and a decision. Feature lists are useful for finding questions, but a published capability is not proof that a product completes the task well. For this first cycle, choose a recurring task, name the useful output and record how much checking it needs. Include the customer's existing assistant, a folder of notes and a manual process among the alternatives.

The retained public records suggest three areas for investigation. First, repeated work has several components: its method, trigger, inputs and delivery. Second, draft and approval controls have boundaries that differ between products, especially when live data remains shared. Third, application subscriptions and model allowances can use different units. Compare a customer's expected recurring use before drawing a conclusion from a headline price.

Evidence health matters throughout the comparison. Keep original links and observation dates, distinguish product claims from firsthand observations, and record when cached material disagrees with a live page. A successful retrieval does not establish that a claim is supported. Human review should reflect an actual check, while unresolved questions and contrary observations remain visible.

The next step is one small task comparison. Prepare the inputs, attempt the same task with two plausible alternatives and retain the useful result, failures and correction effort. Choose act, investigate, watch or ignore in the decision ledger only after reviewing the evidence. Revisit the decision when a material product change, customer request or failed experiment changes its basis.
