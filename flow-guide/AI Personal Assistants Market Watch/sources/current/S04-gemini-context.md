## Retained finding

Connected Google data supports personalization. With Keep Activity enabled, relevant summaries/excerpts/inferences may be used for model improvement; the page distinguishes this from training directly on entire Workspace/Photos libraries. Disconnecting an app does not delete Gemini Apps Activity.

## Limits

account, app, country and settings matter. This is the publisher's policy explanation, not a privacy audit; do not shorten it to either “trains on your entire inbox” or “never trains on private data.”
