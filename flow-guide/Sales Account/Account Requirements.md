## About these inputs

**Fictional Meridian Retail opportunity · Northlight Advisory · 12 September 2026**

The requirements are customer-stated needs in the sample discovery notes. “Confirmed” means the need is confirmed, not that a solution meets it.

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | The customer has explained the need |
| Open | A question remains for the next meeting |
| Deferred | Both sides have placed it outside the initial scope |

Keep stable IDs so a proposal can name its assumptions. The evidence labels refer to the two sections in [[Account Discovery]].
