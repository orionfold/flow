## A change worth inspecting

Compare the named tables in the two dated Markdown packets in `inputs/`. The professional-liability quote changes from **4,480 to 4,620 USD**. Its expiring premium stays 4,200 USD, so the displayed change moves from 280 USD / 6.7% to **420 USD / 10%**. The source note explains the revision; the definition performs the arithmetic.

The timeline contains the team's chosen preparation windows. Those dates are separate from policy renewal dates and do not establish any cancellation or notice requirement.
