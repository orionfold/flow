# Research Playbook

## Start from a decision

Describe your customer and product in Founder Profile. Choose a task whose outcome you can inspect. Separate direct competitors, adjacent products and substitutes. Select a small comparison set before collecting more material.

## Use public sources deliberately

| Source | Useful signal | Check before relying on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official documentation and release notes | Supported behavior and changes | Tier, date, platform and any examples that are only screenshots |
| Pricing and allowance pages | Packaging, limits and cost units | Currency, billing basis, minimum seats, usage and observation date |
| Status history | Operational failures and recovery | Product component, duration and whether the incident affected your task |
| Public repositories | License and inspectable implementation | Visible source facts versus inferred deployed architecture |
| App stores and public customer discussions | Language, complaints and where work goes next | Anecdote versus representative evidence; personal information |
| Public demos and onboarding | Sequence, defaults and friction | Demonstration versus independently completed work |

Open an original page when it matters, keep a short dated paraphrase and link, then add a factual record to Market Inputs. Capture only what you need for the decision. A changed URL can prompt inspection; it does not silently rewrite your factual ledger.

## Keep evidence and judgment separate

Published claims, firsthand execution, retrieved context, citations and human decisions are different facts. Record contrary evidence and unknowns. A coverage chart measures this research set, not market share or product quality.

## Complete a useful cycle

Inspect Jobs and Inputs beside Competitor Watch. Save the inputs, run saved Jobs, review the chart/table changes and proposed expansion, then record a decision. Prepare a separate concise briefing only when useful. Retain your question, next experiment and revisit trigger so a quiet week is still informative.
