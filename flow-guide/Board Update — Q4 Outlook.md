# What we are telling the board about Q4

Q3 closed at 41% growth on the back of six enterprise deals, and the honest
reading is that the number is real but the *motion* behind it is not repeatable
yet. This document says what we expect in Q4, what would have to be true, and
what we would do if it is not.

![The room where the quarter gets argued](assets/boardroom-annual.jpeg)

## The forecast, and its two assumptions

| Line | Q3 actual | Q4 plan | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise | $1.84M | $2.30M | +25% |
| Self-serve | $210k | $340k | +62% |
| Services | $95k | $95k | flat |
| **Total** | **$2.15M** | **$2.74M** | **+27%** |

Two assumptions carry that plan, and both are stated so they can be checked
rather than assumed:

1. **Four of the seven late-stage enterprise deals close.** Three have signed
   verbal commitments; the other four are genuinely open.
2. **Self-serve conversion improves from 1.9% to 2.8%.** This is the one that
   worries us — see [[Quarterly Business Review]] for why the current number
   has not moved in three quarters.

## What we would do if the self-serve number does not move

Nothing dramatic in-quarter. The plan holds without it — enterprise alone
delivers 21% — and reacting to one quarter of a metric we have already
mis-forecast twice would be its own mistake. What changes is the *year* plan,
and that is a January conversation.

> The decision we want from the board is not about Q4. It is whether the
> self-serve investment continues at its current size into next year.

## What we are asking for

- Approval of the Q4 plan as stated.
- A decision by 15 January on the self-serve investment.
- Nothing else. The rest of this is reporting.
