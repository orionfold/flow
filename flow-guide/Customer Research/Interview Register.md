## About these inputs

**Eleven fictional interviews.** One primary need is coded per interview for this demonstration. These invented statements are not customer quotes or market validation.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Id | Stable interview identifier |
| Segment | Enterprise or Self-serve in this sample |
| Need | One researcher-assigned primary code |
| Mentions | 1 per interview; do not count quotations as extra people |

The register has six enterprise and five self-serve entries. Keep consent, original notes and the interview date in your own research records; include only material you may use.
