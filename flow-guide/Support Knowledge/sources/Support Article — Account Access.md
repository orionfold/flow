# Account access

**Fictional Beacon Scheduling help article.** This is not an instruction for Flow or any real external service. Current means approved within the sample story, not published by a Flow job.

Before retrying an invitation, confirm the email address and workspace named in it. Open it with that account selected. If the invitation has expired, ask the workspace owner for a new one. Do not forward another person's invitation.

| Check | What to confirm in this fictional workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Invitation | Email address and workspace match the intended account |
| Selected account | Open the invitation with the invited account selected |
| Expired invitation | Ask the workspace owner for a new invitation |
| Another person's invitation | Do not forward it as a substitute for your own |

Source cases: **SK-01, SK-04, SK-07, SK-10**, in [the initial digest](Support%20Cases%20%E2%80%94%202026-09-05.md).
