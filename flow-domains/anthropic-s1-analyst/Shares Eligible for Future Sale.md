## Class N Common Stock

Shares of our Class N common stock are not listed on a stock exchange and are not registered under the Securities Act. Shares of our Class N common stock will automatically convert into shares of Class A common stock upon a qualifying transfer, which shares would be subject to the resale restrictions and registration rights described in this section. See the section titled “Description of Capital Stock” for a complete description of the conversion mechanics applicable to our Class N common stock.

Following the completion of this offering, shares of Class N common stock will consist of:

• shares, representing shares outstanding as of September 30, 2026 and shares to be issued

immediately prior to the completion of this offering pursuant to the Preferred Stock Conversion with respect to all outstanding shares of our non-voting redeemable convertible preferred stock;

• shares to be issued immediately prior to the completion of this offering pursuant to the

conversion of outstanding convertible promissory notes at the applicable fixed conversion prices, representing shares issuable upon the completion of this offering without causing the holder to exceed any applicable ownership cap;

• shares issuable at certain intervals following the completion of this offering pursuant to the

conversion of any remaining outstanding convertible promissory notes at the applicable fixed conversion prices that could not be converted immediately prior to the completion of this offering without exceeding such ownership cap; and

•any shares issuable in connection with (i) the conversion of any convertible promissory notes that may

be issued under our NPAs following the completion of this offering, (ii) draws under our NPAs that are settled directly in shares of Class N common stock following the completion of this offering, (iii) donations that we match under our Donation Matching Plan, and (iv) certain contractual rights to private placements, whether concurrent with or following the completion of this offering.

For a description of the conversion mechanics of the outstanding notes and the rights to future issuances of notes and Class N common stock, see the section titled “Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations—Liquidity and Capital Resources—Convertible Notes.”

Additionally, certain holders of our Class N common stock are entitled to registration rights with respect to their shares, the exercise of which could facilitate sales of large blocks of our shares into the public market following any applicable lock-up period. See the section titled “Description of Capital Stock—Registration Rights” for a description of these registration rights.

## Rule 144

In general, Rule 144 provides that once we have been subject to the public company reporting requirements of Section 13 or Section 15(d) of the Exchange Act for at least 90 days, a person who is not deemed to have been one of our affiliates for purposes of the Securities Act at any time during the 90 days preceding a sale and who has beneficially owned the shares of our Class A common stock proposed to be sold for at least six months is entitled to sell those shares without complying with the manner of sale, volume limitation, or notice provisions of Rule 144, subject to compliance with the public information requirements of Rule 144. If such a person has beneficially owned the shares proposed to be sold for at least one year, including the holding period of any prior owner other than our affiliates, then that person would be entitled to sell those shares without complying with any of the requirements of Rule 144.

In general, Rule 144 provides that our affiliates or persons selling shares of our Class A common stock on behalf of our affiliates are entitled to sell upon expiration of the market standoff agreements and lock-up agreements

## Rule 701

Rule 701 generally allows a stockholder who purchased shares of our capital stock pursuant to a written compensatory plan or contract and who is not deemed to have been an affiliate of our company during the immediately preceding 90 days to sell these shares in reliance upon Rule 144, but without being required to comply with the public information, holding period, volume limitation, or notice provisions of Rule 144. Rule 701 also permits affiliates of our company to sell their Rule 701 shares under Rule 144 without complying with the holding period requirements of Rule 144. All holders of Rule 701 shares, however, are required to wait until 90 days after the date of this prospectus before selling those shares pursuant to Rule 701.

## Registration Rights

Pursuant to our IRA and the Google RRA, after the completion of this offering, the holders of up to shares of our Class A common stock, or certain transferees, will be entitled to certain rights with respect to the registration of the offer and sale of those shares under the Securities Act. See the section titled “Description of Capital Stock—Registration Rights” for a description of these registration rights. If the offer and sale of these shares of our Class A common stock are registered, the shares will be freely tradable without restriction under the Securities Act, subject to the Rule 144 limitations applicable to affiliates, and a large number of shares may be sold into the public market.

## Registration Statements on Form S-8

We intend to file one or more registration statements on Form S-8 under the Securities Act promptly after the effectiveness of this offering to register shares of our Class A common stock subject to RSUs and options outstanding, as well as reserved for future issuance, under our 2021 Plan, 2026 Plan, and ESPP, as well as the resale of certain shares of our Class A common stock previously issued under our 2021 Plan. Such registration statements on Form S-8 are expected to become effective immediately upon filing, and shares of our Class A common stock covered by such registration statements will then become eligible for sale in the public market, subject to applicable limitations under Rule 144, vesting restrictions, and any applicable market standoff agreements and lock-up agreements. See the section titled “Executive Compensation—Equity Plans” for a description of our equity compensation plans.
