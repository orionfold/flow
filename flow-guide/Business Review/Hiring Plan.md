# Hiring Plan

**Fictional engineering proposal · six roles · approval pending**

## Hire against the bottleneck, then review capacity

The proposed sequence removes single-person dependencies first. Timing is relative to an approved start, not a promise that all six roles will be filled.

| Wave | Role | Work unlocked | Evidence before approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Senior backend engineer | Ingest design has a second owner | Current owner load and design backlog |
| 1 | Design engineer | Prototype and implementation work can progress together | Prioritized customer flows |
| 2 | Backend engineer | Parallel delivery after the ingest design settles | Agreed scope and onboarding capacity |
| 2 | Quality lead | Repeatable release verification | Measured escaped issues and test gaps |
| 3 | Frontend engineer | Reader and editor delivery capacity | Confirmed next-quarter scope |
| 3 | Data engineer | Reporting pipeline ownership | Defined reports and source contracts |

## Cost is a scenario, not an affordability conclusion

An illustrative $1.4M annual loaded cost means about $117k a month once all six roles are filled. Actual in-year cost depends on start dates, compensation, fees and equipment. Revenue alone does not establish available cash.

Use [[Board Outlook]] for the scenario discussion. Before approving a wave, attach the cash plan, total compensation assumptions and hiring owner. Record **approved / held / declined**, the date and the reason in [[Decision Record]].
