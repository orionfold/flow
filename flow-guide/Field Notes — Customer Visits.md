# Field Notes — Customer Visits

**Owner:** sales · **Date:** November 11, 2026 · **Status:** raw notes

Three visits this week, written up on the train home. These are raw and
receive a proper edit before they go in the CRM.
Ths weeks notes contian three typos for the proofread check.

## Meridian Retail — Tuesday

![A retail floor during a customer visit](assets/retail-interior.jpeg)

*Meridian Retail's operations floor — where the approval record mattered more than the feature list.*

The operations team was the real buyer here, not procurement. Their
whole complaint is that documents get approved in one system and stored
in another, so the record of *why* a decision was made is separate from
the decision itself. They have been solving this with a spreadsheet that
one person maintains by hand.

What they liked: the approval record travelling with the document. What
they pushed back on: whether it works when half the team is on Windows.
An honest no was better received than a hedge.

## Larkspur Robotics — Wednesday

Engineering-led, which changed the conversation completely. They wanted
to know where the model runs before they wanted to know what it does.
The answer that landed was that they can point it at their own machines
and nothing leaves the building.

Their inspection reports are the use case — long, structured, and full
of measurements that must not be silently altered. The byte-for-byte
guarantee mattered more here than anywhere else I have pitched.

## Northgate Advisory — Thursday

Smallest of the three and the most likely to buy this quarter. They do
client deliverables under their own brand and the review burden falls on
two partners who are the bottleneck. Anything that makes a review
faster without making it looser is worth money to them.

## What I would change in the pitch

- Lead with where work runs. It is the first question every technical
  buyer asks and answering it early buys credibility for everything after.
- Stop demoing the table editor first. It is impressive and it is not
  what anyone is buying.
- Have a real answer about Windows rather than an apologetic one.
