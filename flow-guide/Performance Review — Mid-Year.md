the roadmap and it landed without a customer-visible incident, which is not the
usual outcome for that kind of work. The development area is unchanged from
January and is about influence rather than execution.

## What went well

**The migration.** A five-month project with a hard cutover and no rollback path
after a certain point. Dan built the reversibility in early, which is why the
one problem that did occur was a twenty-minute fix rather than a weekend.

**Bringing Mei along.** She ran the cutover, and can now maintain the system
alone. That was an explicit goal and it was met properly rather than nominally.

**Judgment under schedule pressure.** In the CMS decision he argued a position,
lost, and implemented the other option well. That is harder than it sounds.

## What to work on

**Writing decisions down as they happen.** Three of five architecture decisions
were written, and the two missing are the ones where the reasoning is now only
in Dan's head. This is the same development area as January.

**Concretely, for the second half:** every architecture decision gets a short
written record within a week of being made — not an essay, a page. Priya reviews
at each one-to-one rather than at the year end.

## Support I owe

- A named reviewer for each decision record so it is a conversation not homework
- Two fewer meetings a week, starting now
