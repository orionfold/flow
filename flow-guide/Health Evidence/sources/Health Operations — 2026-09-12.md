# One more week, the same counting rule

**Fictional operational note.** The completed week beginning 7 September adds **132 scheduled appointments: 119 completed, 7 missed, 6 cancelled**. The outcome counts sum to 132. All five earlier weekly rows are unchanged.

| Week beginning 7 September | Appointments |
| --- | ---: |
| Scheduled | 132 |
| Completed | 119 |
| Missed without cancellation | 7 |
| Cancelled before the appointment | 6 |

The added row's missed share is 5.3%; the six-week pooled share is 7.2%. The point is encouraging enough to discuss, but this is still a small uncontrolled series. Ask whether changes in appointment mix or rescheduling explain part of it before attributing an effect to reminders.
