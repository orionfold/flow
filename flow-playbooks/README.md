# Flow Playbooks

A Flow Playbook is a folder of living documents, Elements and Chat that packages
someone's expertise as a consulting, training or decision tool. Bring one into
Flow with **File ▸ New from GitHub…** and its address; Flow shows the files,
the version and the licence before it adds anything.

| Playbook | What it is | Address | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Consulting Starter](ai-consulting-starter/) | Amazon's mechanisms, Working Backwards and the PR/FAQ, applied to adopting AI, from finding the opportunity to taking a pilot to scale | `github.com/orionfold/flow/tree/main/flow-playbooks/ai-consulting-starter` | CC BY 4.0 |
| [Anthropic S-1 Analyst](anthropic-s1-analyst/) | A guided reading of the filing, with the questions to ask of it. | `github.com/orionfold/flow/tree/main/flow-playbooks/anthropic-s1-analyst` | CC BY 4.0 |

Each Playbook's licence is the one its `README.md` states.
