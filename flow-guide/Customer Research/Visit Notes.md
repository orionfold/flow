# Visit Notes

**Fictional field notes · observation exercise · no real customer statements**

![An imagined retail work setting](assets/retail-interior.jpeg)

## A document handover broke at the approval step

In the Meridian scenario, a merchandising analyst kept the vendor brief in one folder and the approval conversation in another system. During the walkthrough, the reviewer could find the final recommendation but could not identify the assumption that changed.

| Observation | Interpretation | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final brief and discussion stored separately | Handover loses context | Ask to see a recent approved revision |
| Analyst copies a comparison table weekly | Repeated assembly work | Measure time and error rate across two cycles |
| Reviewer asks for the original quotation | Source access affects trust | Add the quotation beside the brief |

## What remains unknown

We have not measured frequency, time saved, willingness to pay, or adoption across other teams. These are research questions, not sales claims.

When adapting this note, keep observations separate from your interpretation. Replace the illustration and sample with material you are entitled to use.
