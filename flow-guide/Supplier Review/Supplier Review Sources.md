## The one commercial change

The **12 September** packet revises Paper Harbor's offered fixed fee from **USD 17,400 to USD 16,200**. Its current fee remains USD 18,000. No other quote or invoice changed. The total offered annual fixed fees move from USD 47,280 to **USD 46,080**, a USD 1,920 reduction against the USD 48,000 current-fee total.

Price alone does not settle the decision. The review brief names missing service-level and scope facts rather than inventing a weighted winner.
