# Description of Capital Stock

## General

We expect to adopt an amended and restated certificate of incorporation and amended and restated bylaws that will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering, and this description summarizes the provisions that are expected to be included in such documents. Because it is only a summary, it does not contain all the information that may be important to you. For a complete description of the matters set forth in this section titled “Description of Capital Stock,” you should refer to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation, amended and restated bylaws, the Trust Agreement, the Founder LLC Agreement, the IRA, and the Stockholders’ Agreement, which are included as exhibits to the registration statement of which this prospectus forms a part, and to the applicable provisions of Delaware law. ## Common Stock

We have four classes of authorized common stock:

•the class designated as Participating Common Stock, which is divided into two series: Class A common

stock and Class N common stock;

•Class T common stock, which is a special class of common stock that is issued to the LTBT;

•Class F common stock, which is a special class of common stock that will be issued to the Founder LLC;

and

•Class E common stock, which is a special class of common stock that will be issued to our employees,

excluding our co-founders.

### Voting Rights

•Class A common stock. Holders of our Class A common stock are entitled to one vote for each share

held on all matters submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the election of the directors other than the Class T directors.

•Class N common stock. Holders of our Class N common stock are not entitled to vote on any matter

that is submitted to a vote of stockholders, except as otherwise required by law, such as certain amendments to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that would adversely affect the powers, preferences, or special rights of our Class N common stock without so adversely affecting our Class A common stock, on which the DGCL entitles such holders to vote separately as a series.

•Class T common stock. The holder of our Class T common stock has no voting rights with respect to

matters submitted to stockholders, except as set forth within our amended and restated certificate of incorporation with respect to the election of a majority of our board of directors, the redemption of our Class E common stock, certain amendments to the rights of our Class F common stock, or as otherwise required by law, such as certain amendments to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that would adversely affect the powers, preferences, or special rights of our Class T common stock, on which the DGCL entitles such holders to vote separately as a class.

•Class F common stock. The holder of our Class F common stock will be entitled to an aggregate

number of votes that equals 50.1% of the total voting power of our outstanding shares of capital stock as of the applicable record date on matters submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the election of the directors other than the Class T directors. Our Class F common stock will be voted in the manner provided by the Founder LLC Agreement. See the section titled “—Class F Common Stock Voting Mechanics.”

•Class E common stock. The holders of our Class E common stock have no voting rights with respect to

matters submitted to stockholders, except as set forth within our amended and restated certificate of incorporation with respect to the proposed nomination, removal, or change in designation of the Specified Class T Director (in circumstances where our NGC does not concur with the LTBT’s proposed action), certain changes to the terms of the LTBT, the rights of our Class T common stock, the redemption of our Class T common stock, or as otherwise required by law, such as certain amendments to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that would adversely affect the powers, preferences, or special rights of our Class E common stock, on which the DGCL entitles such holders to vote separately as a class.

### Conversion and Transferability

•Class A common stock. Our Class A common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Subject to compliance with applicable law and the restrictions described in the section titled “Shares Eligible for Future Sale—Lock-Up and Market Standoff Agreements,” our Class A common stock can be transferred.

•Class N common stock. The holders of our Class N common stock are primarily strategic business

partners. Subject to compliance with applicable law and the restrictions described in the section titled “Shares Eligible for Future Sale—Lock-Up and Market Standoff Agreements,” our Class N common stock can be transferred. The shares of Class N common stock will automatically convert into an equal number of shares of Class A common stock upon a transfer effected through: (i) a registered offering or an open market sale of such shares or (ii) a transfer to a third party (A) that is not a holder of Class N common stock (or an affiliate of a holder of Class N common stock) and (B) that will not beneficially own or control more than 9.99% of the shares of any class or series of our equity securities after giving effect to the transfer.

•Class T common stock. Our Class T common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Our Class T common stock is transferable only upon the approval of persons comprising the Trust Amendment Threshold. Because the persons comprising the Trust Amendment Threshold have the power to modify or terminate the LTBT and the rights of our Class T common stock entirely, there is no separate restriction on the identity of a permissible transferee of our Class T common stock.

•Class F common stock. Our Class F common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Our Class F common stock is not transferable except in the case of a reorganization or redomiciliation of the Founder LLC that is approved by our board of directors and does not result in a change to the members of the Founder LLC.

•Class E common stock. Our Class E common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock and is not transferable.

### Redemption

•Class A common stock. Our Class A common stock will not be redeemable at our option or at the

option of the holder.

•Class N common stock. Our Class N common stock will not be redeemable at our option or at the

option of the holder.

•Class T common stock. Our Class T common stock will be redeemable for no consideration:

members of our board of directors and 80% of the Trustees then in office (in each case, rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up));

◦if our Class F common stock remains outstanding and the Trustees are not voting in favor of such

•until the third anniversary of the completion of this offering, upon approval by (i) our Class F

•at any time, upon approval by (i) our Class F common stock acting by decision of 66 2/3% of

◦if no Class F common stock remains outstanding and the Trustees are not voting in favor of such

•Class F common stock. Our Class F common stock will be redeemable for no consideration (i) at the

option of the Founder LLC, acting upon Supermajority approval of the Continuing Founders, (ii) upon the dissolution of the Founder LLC, or (iii) upon completion of the Class F Transition Period, which will commence when there have been fewer than three Continuing Founders (not including any successor member who is a “Non-Qualifying Member” as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement) for 90 consecutive calendar days.

•Class E common stock. Our Class E common stock will not be redeemable at the option of the holder.

Following an employee’s separation from employment with us other than for cause, one-half of such employee’s then-outstanding shares of Class E common stock will be automatically redeemed by us for no consideration annually, except that all shares of Class E common stock held by an employee will also be automatically redeemed by us for no consideration upon such employee’s death, incapacity, termination for cause, or commencement of service with a direct competitor. All shares of Class E common stock will also be automatically redeemed by us for no consideration (i) at such time as no Class F common stock remains outstanding, or (ii) upon the approval of (A) 80% of authorized members of our board of directors (rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up)), (B) the holder of our Class F common stock, and (C) the holder of our Class T common stock.

### Dividend Rights

Subject to preferences that may apply to any shares of preferred stock outstanding at the time, the holders of our Class A common stock and Class N common stock are entitled to receive dividends out of funds legally available if our board of directors, in its discretion, determines to issue dividends and then only at the times and in the amounts that our board of directors may determine. Our Class T common stock, Class F common stock, and Class E common stock are not entitled to receive dividends. See the section titled “Dividend Policy” for more information.

### No Preemptive or Similar Rights

Our common stock is not entitled to preemptive rights and is not subject to redemption or sinking fund provisions (other than those providing for the redemption of our Class T common stock, Class F common stock, and Class E common stock for no consideration described above).

### Right to Receive Liquidation Distributions

If we become subject to a liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up, the assets legally available for distribution to our stockholders would be distributable ratably among the holders of our Class A common stock and Class N common stock and any participating preferred stock outstanding at that time, subject to prior satisfaction of all outstanding debt and liabilities and the preferential rights of and the payment of liquidation preferences, if any,

In the event of our liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up, holders of our Class T common stock, Class F common stock, and Class E common stock shall be entitled to receive par value ($0.00001) per share but shall not otherwise participate in such distributions.

### Fully Paid and Non-Assessable

In connection with this offering, our legal counsel will opine that the shares of our Class A common stock to be issued in this offering will be fully paid and non-assessable.

## Preferred Stock

After the completion of this offering, no shares of our preferred stock will be outstanding. Pursuant to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering, our board of directors (if 80% of the authorized members of our board of directors (rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up)) approve) will have the authority, subject to limitations set forth in such amended and restated certificate of incorporation or prescribed by Delaware law, to issue preferred stock in one or more series, to establish from time to time the number of shares to be included in each series and to fix the designation, powers, preferences, and rights of the shares of each series and any of its qualifications, limitations, or restrictions, in each case without further vote or action by our stockholders; provided that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, our board of directors shall not designate or issue any series of preferred stock without the affirmative vote or consent of the Founder LLC. Our board of directors (if 80% of the authorized members of our board of directors (rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up)) approve) can also increase or decrease the number of shares of any series of preferred stock, but not below the number of shares of that series then outstanding, without any further vote or action by our stockholders. Subject to limitations set forth in our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering, or prescribed by Delaware law, we may authorize the issuance of preferred stock with voting or conversion rights that could adversely affect the voting power or other rights of the holders of our common stock, including the likelihood that such holders will receive dividend payments and payments upon liquidation. If we were to issue shares of preferred stock with the right to vote on matters submitted to our stockholders generally, the voting power of our outstanding Class A common stock would be diluted, while the voting power of our Class F common stock would remain fixed at an aggregate of 50.1% of the total voting power of our outstanding shares of capital stock as of the applicable record date with respect to such matters. ## Anti-Takeover Provisions

Certain provisions of Delaware law, our amended and restated certificate of incorporation and our amended and restated bylaws, which will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering, which are summarized below, may have the effect of delaying, deferring, or discouraging another person from acquiring control of us. They are also designed, in part, to encourage persons seeking to acquire control of us to negotiate first with our board of directors. We believe that the benefits of increased protection of our potential ability to negotiate with an unsolicited acquirer outweigh the disadvantages of discouraging a proposal to acquire us because negotiation of these proposals could result in an improvement of their terms.

### Delaware Law

We have elected not to be governed by the provisions of Section 203 of the DGCL, prior to the later to occur of the Class F Sunset Time and the Class T Sunset Time, but our amended and restated certificate of incorporation will provide that the restrictions contained in Section 203 will apply to us immediately following the later to occur of either the Class F Sunset Time or the Class T Sunset Time. Following the later to occur of the Class F Sunset Time or the Class T Sunset Time, we will thereafter be governed by Section 203, which generally prohibits a public Delaware corporation from engaging in a “business combination” with an “interested stockholder” for a period of three years after the date of the transaction in which the person became an interested stockholder, unless:

•the business combination or transaction which resulted in the stockholder becoming an interested

stockholder was approved by the board of directors prior to the time that the stockholder became an interested stockholder;

•upon consummation of the transaction which resulted in the stockholder becoming an interested

stockholder, the interested stockholder owned at least 85% in voting power of the voting stock of the corporation outstanding at the time the transaction commenced, excluding shares owned by directors who are also officers of the corporation and shares owned by employee stock plans in which employee participants do not have the right to determine confidentially whether shares held subject to the plan will be tendered in a tender or exchange offer; or

•at or subsequent to the time the stockholder became an interested stockholder, the business

combination was approved by the board of directors and authorized at an annual or special meeting of the stockholders, and not by written consent, by the affirmative vote of at least two-thirds of the outstanding voting stock which is not owned by the interested stockholder.

In general, Section 203 defines a “business combination” to include mergers, asset sales, and other transactions resulting in financial benefit to a stockholder and an “interested stockholder” as a person who, together with affiliates and associates, owns, or is an affiliate or associate of the corporation and within three years did own, 15% or more in voting power of the corporation’s outstanding voting stock.

### Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation and Amended and Restated Bylaws Provisions

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation and our amended and restated bylaws, which will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering, will include a number of provisions that could deter hostile takeovers or delay or prevent changes in control of our board of directors or management team, including but not limited to the following:

#### Multi-Class Common Stock

As described above in the section titled “—Common Stock—Voting Rights,” our amended and restated certificate of incorporation provides for a multi-class common stock structure. Under this governance structure, we expect that the LTBT and the Founder LLC will collectively have the ability to elect all members of our board of directors, and the Founder LLC will have the ability to determine the outcome of all matters submitted to a vote of our stockholders generally for the foreseeable future, including the approval by our stockholders of significant corporate transactions such as a merger or other sale of our company or its assets. Such transactions also require approval of our board of directors, a majority of whom are elected by the LTBT. Holders of our Class A common stock generally will not be able to affect the outcome of any matter submitted to a vote of our stockholders or the election of our directors, regardless of how they vote, unless a majority of the Continuing Founders cannot agree on how our Class F common stock will be voted (in which case our Class F common stock will generally be voted proportionately with the votes of our Class A common stock cast on the matter).

#### Classified Board

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the completion of this offering will provide that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, we will not have a classified board of directors, and all directors will be elected for annual terms. Each director’s term will continue until the next annual meeting and until the election and qualification of his or her successor, or his or her earlier death, resignation, or removal.

Following the Class F Sunset Time, our board of directors will shift to having a classified board of directors consisting of three classes of approximately equal size, each serving staggered three-year terms. At such time, our directors would be assigned by the then-current board of directors to a class. If our Class T common stock remains outstanding, the Class T directors and the remaining directors shall be allocated among the classes so that each class includes a proportionate number of Class T directors and other directors. Upon expiration of the term of a class of directors, directors for that class would each be elected for a three-year term at the annual meeting of stockholders in the year in which that term expires. As a result, only one class of directors would be elected at each annual meeting of our stockholders, with the other classes continuing for the remainder of their respective three-year terms. Each director’s term would continue until the third annual meeting of stockholders following their election and until his or her successor has been elected and qualified, or until his or her earlier death, resignation, or removal. Any increase or decrease in the number of directors would be distributed among the three classes so that, as nearly as practicable, each class would consist of one-third of our directors.

#### Board Vacancies

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation and amended and restated bylaws will provide that (i) prior to the Class T Sunset Time, only the holder of our Class T common stock can fill vacant directorships of any Class T director, subject to the nomination procedures for the Specified Class T Director; and (ii) prior to the Class F Sunset Time, vacant or newly created directorships for any directors other than our Class T directors can be filled by (a) our board of directors, in accordance with, and following receipt of written notice from, the Founder LLC recommending the person proposed to fill such director seat or (b) the holders of our Class A common stock and Class F common stock, voting together or acting by written consent. Following the Class F Sunset Time, only our board of directors will be permitted to fill vacant or newly created directorships for any directors other than our Class T directors. Prior to the Class T Sunset Time, the number of directors constituting our board of directors will be permitted to be set only with the approval of 80% of authorized members of our board of directors (rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up)); provided that until the earlier to occur of the Class F Sunset Time or the Class T Sunset Time, such total number of authorized directors must be an odd number. Following the Class T Sunset Time, the number of

directors constituting our board of directors will be permitted to be set with the approval of a majority of the authorized members of our board of directors. These provisions would prevent a stockholder from increasing the size of our board of directors and then gaining control of our board of directors by filling the resulting vacancies with its own nominees. This will make it more difficult to change the composition of our board of directors.

Our amended and restated bylaws will provide advance notice procedures for stockholders, other than holders of Class T common stock or Class F common stock nominating directors in accordance with nomination procedures in our certificate of incorporation or the Stockholders’ Agreement, seeking to bring business before our annual meeting of stockholders or to nominate candidates for election as directors at a meeting of stockholders. The DGCL provides that stockholders are not entitled to cumulate votes in the election of directors unless a corporation’s certificate of incorporation provides otherwise. Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation does not provide for cumulative voting.
