# Research Map

Add a diagram that explains your research process. The diagram describes the work; it does not schedule or execute its steps.

## Research method

Start by choosing a customer task and a decision. Inspect a relevant public source, retain a dated capture, and extract a finding with its limits. Refresh the comparison views from those findings. Review proposed analysis beside its evidence, decide the next small experiment, and return when a material source or customer need changes.
