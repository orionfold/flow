# Account Discovery

**Fictional discovery notes · no real customer statements**

## Discovery 01 — 5 September

Mara owns the weekly trading summary; Elliot prepares vendor briefs. They describe fourteen recurring documents, with five proposed for the initial pilot. Their next task is to establish baseline effort and error rates.

| Person | Role | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mara | Merchandising lead | A review she can complete before Monday trading |
| Elliot | Analyst | One source set for recurring briefs |
| Nina | Legal reviewer | A document she can inspect in her existing tools |

## Discovery 02 — 12 September

Nina asks how counsel can receive a reviewable export. Mara adds that some reviewers use Windows. These are unresolved constraints, not features we may promise.

**Next meeting:** demonstrate the actual handover and identify the five pilot documents. Do not mark either question resolved from a product description alone.
