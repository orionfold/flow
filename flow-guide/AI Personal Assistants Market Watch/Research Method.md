# Research Method

Generate a diagram that explains the research process. The diagram describes choices; it does not execute, schedule or authorize them.

## Evidence to decision

Choose a research question and audience. Select permitted inputs. Inspect an original public source and retain a dated claim with its limits. Compare it with the existing ledger. If the change is material, update saved findings and refresh the views; if not, record no material change. Review analysis against the evidence. Decide investigate, act, watch or ignore. Prepare a private briefing only when an audience needs it. Return when a stated event or failed assumption changes the basis.

## Review before saving

| Check | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Steps match the prose | Keep collection, comparison, review, decision and return. |
| No-change path remains | Quiet evidence must not become an invented event. |
| No hidden automation | A diagram arrow does not run another job. |
